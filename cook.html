Fries

Ingredients

  • Ingredients List 3 large Russet potatoes (or baking potatoes) 2 tablespoons olive oil 0.5 teaspoon salt 0.25 teaspoon garlic powder (optional)

Method

1. Prep the Oven Heat the oven to 210°C (425°F). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper. [1, 2, 3] 2. Cut into Fries Wash and scrub the potatoes thoroughly. Slice the potatoes into long, 1 cm (\(\frac{1}{2}\text{-inch}\)) thick sticks. Tip: An adult should supervise or help with the sharp knife during this step. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5] 3. Soak and Dry [1] Soak the potato sticks in a bowl of cold water for 10 minutes to remove excess starch. Drain the water and dry the potatoes completely with a clean kitchen towel. Note: Drying them thoroughly is the secret to making them truly crispy. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5] 4. Season and Toss • Place the dry potatoes on the baking sheet. • Drizzle with the olive oil. • Sprinkle the salt and garlic powder over them. • Toss with clean hands until every fry is lightly coated in oil and spices. Spread them out so they do not overlap. [1, 2, 3, 4] 5. Bake Until Golden Bake for 20 to 25 minutes. Use a spatula to flip them halfway through the cooking time. Remove them when they are golden brown and crispy at the edges. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5] .

Pizza

Ingredients

  • Ingredients For the Dough: • \(1 \frac{1}{3}\) cups warm water (100°F to 110°F) • 1 packet (2 \(\frac{1}{4}\) teaspoons) instant yeast • 1 tablespoon granulated sugar • \(3 \frac{1}{2}\) to 4 cups unbleached all-purpose flour (or bread flour for extra chew) • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil • 1 teaspoon salt [1] For the Sauce & Toppings: • 1 cup store-bought or homemade pizza sauce (crushed tomatoes with a pinch of salt) • 2 cups (8 oz) shredded low-moisture mozzarella cheese • 1 tablespoon olive oil (for brushing the crust) • Optional: Pepperoni, fresh basil, sliced bell peppers, or onions [1]

Method

1. Make the Dough In a large bowl, whisk the warm water, sugar, and yeast together. Let it sit for 5 minutes until it becomes frothy. Add the flour, olive oil, and salt. Mix until the dough forms a cohesive, slightly sticky ball. [1, 2, 3, 4] 2. Knead and Rise Knead the dough on a lightly floured surface for 5 to 7 minutes until it is smooth and elastic. Lightly oil a large bowl, place the dough inside, cover it with a warm towel or plastic wrap, and let it rise in a warm spot for 1 to 1.5 hours, or until it has doubled in size. [1, 2, 3, 4] 3. Shape the Pizza Preheat your oven to 475°F (245°C). If using a pizza stone, preheat it with the oven. Punch down the dough to release air bubbles, then divide it in half (this recipe makes two 12-inch pizzas). On a lightly floured surface, stretch or roll the dough out to your desired thickness. [1, 2, 3] 4. Assemble and Bake Transfer the dough to a parchment-lined or greased baking sheet. Brush the edges with a little olive oil. Spread a thin layer of sauce—leaving a half-inch border for the crust—top with mozzarella cheese, and add your toppings. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, or until the crust is golden-brown and the cheese is bubbly. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]
